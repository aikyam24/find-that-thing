A few useful details

Your key is held in extension storage and used by the service worker. Saved pages live in IndexedDB. This is local storage, not an encrypted vault or cloud backup.

Search queries are kept for this browser session. “This is the one” does not send feedback anywhere. Removing the extension can delete the collection.

Forgetting a page removes this extension’s copy and search data. It does not modify browser history or retract excerpts already sent to TypeSafe. Check TypeSafe’s current policies before sending sensitive content.
